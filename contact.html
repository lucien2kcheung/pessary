Getting fitted關於配戴 A pessary needs a pelvic examination and a fitting trial with a doctor or specialist nurse. If you'd like, tell us your area and we'll help point you to a clinic or referral pathway. 配戴子宮托需要由醫生或專科護士進行盆腔檢查及試戴。如有需要，請告知您所在的地區，我們可協助引導您前往診所或轉介途徑。

Not sure which type suits you? Try the short guide on the Types page first — then bring the result to your fitting. 不確定哪款適合您？可先試用類型頁面的簡短指引，再將結果帶到配戴時與醫護人員討論。

In urgent discomfort?出現緊急不適？

This site can't handle medical emergencies. If you have severe pain, bleeding, a pessary you can't remove, or other urgent symptoms, contact your doctor or local emergency services.本網站無法處理醫療緊急情況。如您出現劇痛、出血、無法取出子宮托或其他緊急症狀，請聯絡您的醫生或當地緊急服務。
